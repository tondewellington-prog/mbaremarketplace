Welcome to Mbare Affiliate Program

Earn money by inviting sellers to join Mbare Marketplace! When a seller you refer subscribes to a paid plan, you earn commission.

  • 20% commission on every referral
  • Lifetime recurring earnings
  • Unlock higher tiers with more referrals

How It Works

  • Share your unique referral link
  • Sellers click your link and sign up
  • When they subscribe, you earn commission
  • Earnings are paid monthly via PayNow

Affiliate Tiers

  • Bronze: 100-500 referrals - 20% commission
  • Silver: 501-1000 referrals + 10 x $5 tier - 25% commission
  • Gold: 1000+ referrals + 100 x $5 tier - 30% commission

Leaderboard & Rewards

Top affiliates are featured on our leaderboard. Higher tiers unlock better commission rates and exclusive bonuses.
