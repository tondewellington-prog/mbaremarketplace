Welcome to Mbare Marketplace

Your guide to the platform

Welcome Aboard!

You're now part of Mbare Marketplace, Zimbabwe's growing online shopping destination. Let's take a quick tour to help you get started.

Your Account: Top right corner - manage your profile, orders, and settings.

Find Anything

Use the Search Bar at the top to find products instantly.

Where: Top of the page, next to the Mbare logo.

Or browse by category using the "What do you want to buy today?" dropdown menu.

Where: In the navigation bar below the search bar.

Start Selling

Have products to sell? Click the Sell button to register as a seller and start listing your products.

Where: In the navigation bar, look for the "Sell" link.

You'll get a 1-month free trial of the Merchant Basic plan!

Track Your Performance

Access the Analytics dashboard to track your product views, sales, and customer engagement.

Where: In the navigation bar, click "Analytics".

Analytics is available for paid subscribers.

Earn with Affiliates

Join our Affiliate Program and earn commissions by referring new sellers to the platform.

Where: Scroll to the bottom of the page and find "Become an Affiliate" in the footer.

Earn $0.10 per active seller every month!

Ready to Buy?

Add products to your Basket and proceed to checkout when you're ready.

Where: Top right corner - the basket icon shows your item count.

Happy shopping on Mbare Marketplace!
