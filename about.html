About Mbare Marketplace

Bridging Sellers & Buyers, One Connection at a Time

Our Story

Mbare Marketplace started with a simple idea: what if sellers and buyers could connect directly? No middlemen. No barriers. Just real conversations between real people.

Born from the vibrant energy of Mbare, we built a platform where sellers can share their products and stories, and buyers can discover unique items while connecting directly with the people behind them.

Today, Mbare Marketplace connects sellers and buyers across the globe—all through the power of direct conversation on WhatsApp and a marketplace built on trust.

👨‍💼

Tonderai Wellington Nyamandi
